About GeoFinancer

Player name

Your name and institution appear on the high score list. You only need to enter them once on this device; your best score is the one that counts.

Playing as a guest? You can skip the name, but your scores won't be recorded.

How to play

  1. Look around. You're dropped on a street in front of a real commercial property somewhere in the U.S. Its name is in the top-left corner. Drag to look around; click the arrows on the street to move.
  2. Answer the three questions (bottom right). Two ask where the property's state ranks on something, like business climate, taxes, walkability or climate risk (1 = best or highest). The third asks for a past 30-year mortgage rate or how much that state's home prices changed in a past year.
  3. Drop a pin on the map (bottom left) where you think the property is.
  4. Lock in once everything is answered. You can do it in any order.
  5. Beat the clock. You get 60 seconds per round. The timer turns red at 20 seconds and the alarm sounds at 10. When time runs out, only what you've answered counts.

Each round is worth up to 5,000 points for location and 1,000 for each question. Five rounds; a perfect game is 40,000. The closer you are, the more you score.
